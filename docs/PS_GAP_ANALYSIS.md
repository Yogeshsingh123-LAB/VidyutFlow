# PS000125 — Blunt Evaluator Gap Analysis

> **Purpose:** This document gives evaluators a direct, line-by-line assessment of what **VidyutFlow** actually delivers in working code versus what remains simulated, synthetic, unverified at scale, or out of scope against the official **Uttar Gujarat Vij Company Limited (UGVCL)** problem statement (`PS000125`, verbatim text in [`docs/PS000125_OFFICIAL.md`](PS000125_OFFICIAL.md)).

---

## Requirement-by-Requirement Gap Matrix

| # | PS Requirement (verbatim / official PS000125 scope) | Status | Evidence (Verified in Repository) | Gap |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Unified ERP & portal ecosystem** (*"Modern, Integrated ERP and Portal Ecosystem — Unified platform connecting consumer services, employee workflows, field operations, and management reporting"*) | **Partial** | 5 role-based portals (`frontend/src/components/{consumer,employee,field,management,admin}/`) + configurable workflow engine (`backend/app/workflow.py`) + 50-path `/v1` FastAPI backend (`backend/app/routers/`). | SAP S/4HANA IS-U, e-Urja CIS Billing, and GUVNL 1912 CRM connectors are in-repo mocks (`is_simulated: true` in `backend/app/routers/erp.py`); not connected to any live UGVCL system. |
| 2 | **Eliminate manual data re-entry** (*"Elimination of repetitive manual data entry across systems and departments"* / *"Seamless integration across billing, metering, CRM, asset, finance, and service modules"*) | **Partial** | Single canonical entry across VidyutFlow's 5 portals (`0` applicant fields re-typed across internal staff stages; [`docs/WORKLOAD_MEASUREMENT.md`](WORKLOAD_MEASUREMENT.md)). | Cross-system sync (`POST /v1/erp/gateway/propagate/{id}`) and drift reconciliation (`GET /v1/erp/reconcile`, `POST /v1/erp/reconcile/{id}`) run strictly against simulated in-repo mock state (`is_simulated: true`); no live SAP or e-Urja master-data reconciliation. |
| 3 | **Automated workflow routing & approvals** (*"End-to-End Workflow Automation — Automated validation, prioritization, routing, assignment, escalation, and closure of requests"*) | **Delivered (prototype scope)** | 5 seeded workflow templates (`new_connection`, `load_extension`, `meter_replacement`, `billing_dispute`, `outage_safety`) + Admin custom templates, role-enforced transition matrix, least-loaded zone assignment, and SLA timers (`backend/app/workflow.py`, `backend/app/routers/workflows.py`). | Single-process application logic; not load-tested at UGVCL subdivision/DISCOM concurrency, and multi-tier circle/corporate financial delegation rules are simplified. |
| 4 | **Field mobility & offline operations** (*"Smart Field Operations Support — Mobile-friendly and low-connectivity/offline-capable workflows for field inspections, updates, and task execution"*) | **Delivered (basic)** | Dual-persisted `IndexedDB` + `localStorage` queue (`frontend/src/utils/offlineFieldSync.ts`) + RFC-4122 `client_uuid` server `UNIQUE` dedupe (`POST /v1/requests/field-sync` in `backend/app/routers/requests.py`) + auto-replay on reconnect + local `CONFLICT` capture on HTTP `409`/`403`. | No scheduled exponential backoff timer (retries wait for next `online` event or manual sync), no version-vector / base-timestamp concurrent-edit merge (compatible-state `meter_serial`/`notes` edits are last-writer-wins), no officer-side conflict UI, and no multi-record atomic rollback (see [`docs/OFFLINE.md`](OFFLINE.md)). |
| 5 | **AI-driven decision support** (*"AI-Enabled Smart Services — Intelligent assistance for request classification, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance/load insights, consumer query handling, and decision support"*) | **Partial** | 5 `scikit-learn` pipelines wired into live API endpoints (`backend/app/ml/`) + local `TF-IDF` policy RAG (`/v1/assistant/chat`) + optional `3.5s`-bounded server Gemini + human-only approval gate (`models_can_approve_or_reject = False`). | All 5 models are trained on synthetic data (`backend/app/ml/synthetic.py`) and evaluated on synthetic / team-authored OOD sets; none are trained or validated on real UGVCL historical tickets, MRI billing logs, or feeder meter telemetry. |
| 6 | **Real-time executive visibility** (*"Real-Time Visibility & Analytics — Dashboards for SLA monitoring, operational bottlenecks, service turnaround time, workload balance, and infrastructure health"*) | **Partial** | Live SQL-aggregated KPIs, zone SLA risk watchlist, and executive CSV export over the prototype database (`ManagementPortal.tsx`, `/v1/requests/kpis/executive-analytics`, 45-second polling). | Polling-based (`45 s`), backed only by prototype request rows; zero live 11kV feeder SCADA, DTR IoT sensor, or smart-meter AMI telemetry ingestion. |
| 7 | **Multilingual citizen access** (*"User-Friendly Experience — Intuitive, multilingual, accessible, and responsive interfaces for consumers, office staff, field teams, and administrators"*) | **Delivered** | 4-language UI parity across English (`en`), Gujarati (`gu`), Hindi (`hi`), and Marathi (`mr`) verified by CI tests (`frontend/tests/i18n-parity.test.ts`). | Machine/team-authored translations; terminology has not been vetted by native UGVCL subdivision clerks, linemen, or GERC legal staff, and SMS/email citizen notifications are simulated (`SIMULATED_SMS`). |
| 8 | **Cybersecurity & data protection** (*"Strong Cybersecurity Framework — Role-based access control, secure authentication, encryption, audit trails, threat monitoring, and compliance readiness"*) | **Partial (app-layer)** | Server RBAC, `bcrypt`, `HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax` cookies, header magic-byte checks (`%PDF-`, PNG, JPEG, UTF-8), `VFENC1` HKDF-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM upload vault (`uploads_encrypted/`), SHA-256 hash-chained `AuditLog`, CSP/HSTS headers, and `CEF:0` SIEM export (`backend/app/{security,uploads,audit}.py`). | Application-layer only: secrets derive from env `SECRET_KEY` (no HSM/KMS), no WAF/network VLAN isolation, no ClamAV/CDR deep malware scanning, CSP retains `'unsafe-inline'` in `style-src` for Tailwind runtime styles, and zero external VAPT or CERT-In audit. |
| 9 | **Backup, DR & high availability** (*"Robust Data Backup & Disaster Recovery — Automated backups, replication, failover readiness, and fast recovery mechanisms with minimal downtime"*) | **Minimal** | Local SQLite snapshot script (`backup.sh`), local directory copy + SHA-256 manifest (`offsite_replicate.sh`), 1-byte tamper check (`restore_drill.sh`), and single-host file-restore drill (`cold_start_dr_proof.py`, `POST /v1/system/dr-failover-drill`). | Local single-host SQLite file copy/swap only — not geo-redundant replication, no multi-AZ standby, no PostgreSQL streaming failover tested, and no RPO/RTO measured under concurrent production traffic. |
| 10 | **IT infrastructure & network modernisation** (*"Future-Ready IT Infrastructure — Scalable, resilient, cloud/hybrid-ready architecture with optimized server, network, storage, and edge support"*) | **Not delivered** | Written architecture & vendor-sourced BOM documents only (`docs/INFRA_PROPOSAL.md`, `docs/INFRASTRUCTURE.md`, `docs/INFRASTRUCTURE_BOM_SOURCES.md`) + local `docker-compose.yml`. | Zero physical, edge, SD-WAN/MPLS, cloud VPC, Kubernetes, or rack-server infrastructure is provisioned or tested (see [`docs/INFRA_PROPOSAL.md`](INFRA_PROPOSAL.md)). |
| 11 | **Scalability to utility load** (*"Faster service delivery and reduced turnaround time"* across 100+ UGVCL subdivisions & millions of consumers) | **Not verified** | SQLite default runtime; PostgreSQL 16 and Redis 7 wired in `docker-compose.yml`; rolling in-process latency tracker (`GET /v1/system/performance`). | Zero k6/Locust load or stress benchmarks executed; PostgreSQL connection pooling (PgBouncer) not load-tested; Redis login rate-limiter tested only against an in-memory fake Redis client in `pytest`. |

## Overall Honest Assessment

Overall honest assessment: VidyutFlow delivers a strong workflow + portal + application-security prototype covering roughly 45–55% of PS000125's software scope and 0% of its physical/network infrastructure scope.
