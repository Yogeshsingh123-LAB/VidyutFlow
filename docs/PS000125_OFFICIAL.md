## Description of the Problem (12 points, verbatim substance)

1. Repetitive data entry and activities across multiple systems and portals.
2. Limited integration between ERP, portals and applications, causing duplicate work and manual data movement.
3. Users may have to navigate different interfaces, workflows and authentication processes.
4. Routine activities that can be automated may still require manual intervention.
5. System response, connectivity or processing delays can reduce productivity.
6. IT hardware and infrastructure require continuous modernization and scalability.
7. Security controls must protect data while retaining legitimate and practical accessibility.
8. Lack of intelligent alerts, workflow tracking and proactive assistance increases follow-up work.
9. Need for reliable online/offline working and secure synchronization during connectivity issues.
10. Need for stronger backup, redundancy, disaster recovery and cybersecurity.
11. Management and field users require consolidated, timely and actionable information.
12. The ecosystem must remain compatible with AI, automation, APIs, analytics and future technologies.

The proposed solution should make technology assist the user rather than make the user repeatedly
operate and manage the technology — simple, intuitive, fast, secure interfaces with maximum
automation, intelligent workflows, system integration, alerts, validations and minimum repetitive
data entry. The objective is a **“click-and-get-work-done”** environment: no unnecessary waiting,
no repeated entry of the same information, no navigating multiple systems for the same task, and
no manual performance of automatable activities — with support for AI-assisted decision support,
intelligent automation, APIs, analytics, mobile access and predictive services.

## Users

**Primary:** UGVCL Corporate, Circle, Division and Subdivision employees · Engineers, technical,
administrative and commercial staff · Field staff and supervisory officers · IT/ITeS/system
administrators · Management and decision-making authorities.
**Secondary:** Electricity consumers and applicants · Contractors and authorized agencies ·
Government departments and other authorized stakeholders.

## Expected Outcomes (14)

1. Simple, user-friendly, integrated access to relevant ERP systems and portals.
2. Maximum automation of routine and repetitive activities.
3. “Enter once, use everywhere” wherever technically and legally feasible.
4. Intelligent workflows, alerts, reminders, validations, recommendations and status tracking.
5. Fast response with minimum waiting after a user initiates a process.
6. Reduced manual data entry, duplicate work and follow-up.
7. Secure API-based integration and common data exchange.
8. Modern, scalable PCs, servers, networks, communication devices/modems, storage and backup.
9. Strong cybersecurity, access control, monitoring and disaster recovery.
10. Future compatibility with AI, analytics, automation, cloud/hybrid and emerging technologies.
11. Suitable offline/low-connectivity working with secure synchronization.
12. Real-time dashboards and actionable information.
13. Reduced employee workload and improved productivity.
14. Faster, simpler and more transparent consumer service.

## Potential Impact (summary)

Transform IT from a system users must operate into an intelligent digital assistant: less repetitive
work and follow-up, better availability/performance/cybersecurity, faster and more transparent
consumer service, and a long-term path to a future-ready, citizen-centric digital utility.
