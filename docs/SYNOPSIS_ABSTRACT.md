**Problem ID:** `PS000125` · **Industry:** Uttar Gujarat Vij Company Limited (UGVCL)
**Title:** Future-Ready, Secure and Intelligent IT Infrastructure & User-Friendly ERP/Portal Ecosystem for Automated and Hassle-Free Power Utility Services
**Official Text:** [`PS000125_OFFICIAL.md`](PS000125_OFFICIAL.md) · **Full Mapping:** [`REQUIREMENT_MAPPING.md`](REQUIREMENT_MAPPING.md) · **Infra BOM:** [`INFRASTRUCTURE.md`](INFRASTRUCTURE.md)

**Problem.** UGVCL subdivision workflows suffer from repetitive manual data entry across disconnected portals, SAP finance/work orders, e-Urja billing, and 1912 grievance queues; delayed routing; rural cellular dead-zones stalling field inspections; reactive SLA tracking; and infrastructure/DR/cybersecurity modernization pressure across 100+ subdivisions.

**Solution Architecture (Prototype Scope).** VidyutFlow is a utility workflow prototype with **5 role-isolated multilingual portals** (`en`/`gu`/`hi`/`mr`), **5 seeded configurable utility workflows** (plus Admin-extensible templates), a **50-path / 55-operation `/v1` FastAPI control plane**, and **ERP integration (simulated)** via a **SAP-shaped OData v4 mock (simulated; not connected to SAP)** plus a **3-System fan-out mock (simulated)** across mock SAP FI-CA/IS-U, e-Urja CIS Billing, and GUVNL 1912 Grievance CRM targets (`is_simulated: true`) with in-repo simulated reconciliation. It embeds **5 synthetic-trained `scikit-learn` models** (Category Triage, Severity + Safety Guardrail, Calibrated SLA Breach Predictor, `IsolationForest` Billing Anomaly + GERC RGPU Slab Verifier, and `GradientBoostingRegressor` 24h Feeder/DTR kVA Forecaster), **local TF-IDF policy RAG + optional server-side Gemini**, **idempotent offline field sync (`client_uuid`; see [`OFFLINE.md`](OFFLINE.md))**, **application-layer security + backup script (infra hardening out of scope)** (`%PDF-`/PNG/JPEG/UTF-8 header magic-byte checks + always-on `VFENC1` HKDF-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM vault `uploads_encrypted/`, ArcSight/Wazuh `CEF:0` SIEM export, and local SHA-256 SQLite backup/restore drill scripts), plus a separate **hardware/network modernisation proposal (not delivered)** in [`INFRA_PROPOSAL.md`](INFRA_PROPOSAL.md).
