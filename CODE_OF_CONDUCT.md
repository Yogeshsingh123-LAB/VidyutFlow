# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are a 5-member student team building VidyutFlow for the Seva First Innovation Challenge. We pledge a
respectful, harassment-free collaboration for everyone involved — in issues, pull requests, reviews,
and team discussions — regardless of experience level or background.

## Our standards

**Expected:** be kind and constructive; critique code, not people; credit others' work; raise blockers
early (a stuck teammate told on day 1 costs nothing, on demo day costs everything); keep the
live-vs-simulated honesty rule — we do not oversell the work, to judges or to each other.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, or exclusion; publishing others' private information; knowingly
committing secrets, real consumer data, or fabricated results.

## Scope & enforcement

This applies to all project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, team chats used for the project). The team
leader (@Yogeshsingh123-LAB) is the first contact for concerns; reports are handled confidentially and
in good faith. Consequences range from a private conversation to removal from the project, proportional
to the behaviour.
